Observal Inc. sets aside a fixed monthly budget to thank people who do sustained, meaningful work on Agentic Resource Registry. This page explains how that works. Payments are tracked in public on our [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/observal).

## This is not a salary

Payments from this collective are recognition for contributions, not salary, wages, or a retainer. Receiving a payment doesn't create an employment or contractor relationship with Observal Inc., and it isn't guaranteed to repeat. Amounts and eligibility can change or stop at any time, and the monthly budget is fixed. Please don't rely on it as income.

This is true even if you were paid last month, or for the last ten months.

## How it works

Observal Inc. (a Delaware C-corp) sponsors the project through an Open Collective collective, and is its own fiscal host. The money comes from Observal Inc. and the budget is fixed, about $4,000 USD per month in total.

Open Collective is our public ledger and approval workflow. It is not how the money moves. Once an expense is approved, Observal Inc. pays you by international bank wire and marks the expense as "paid manually" in Open Collective.

## Who can file an expense

Filing is by invitation only.

Maintainers invite contributors after a stretch of sustained, meaningful merged work. We do it this way to prevent abuse, and so maintainers don't get a constant stream of requests.

- Please don't ask for an invite in issues, PRs, Discord, or DMs.
- Expenses from people who weren't invited are rejected without discussion.
- An invite can be withdrawn.

Being invited isn't a promise of a payment, and not being invited isn't a judgement of your work. Keep contributing, and keep the PRs coming.

## How to file

File one expense per month, after the month ends. Title it:

`Observal Contributions (Month YYYY)`

For example: `Observal Contributions (March 2026)`.

Each expense must include:

1. **A link to your merged PRs for the period.** Use a GitHub search filtered by author and dates, for example:
   `https://github.com/AgenticResourceRegistry/AgenticResourceRegistry/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+author%3AYOUR-USERNAME+merged%3A2026-03-01..2026-03-31`
2. **Your GitHub username.**
3. **Your legal name and address.**
4. **Your bank details:** IBAN or account number, SWIFT/BIC, and the account holder name exactly as it appears at your bank.

### Tax form

Before your first payment you must submit a tax form: a **W-8BEN** if you're outside the US, or a **W-9** if you're in the US. Open Collective will prompt you for it. You are responsible for your own taxes. Observal Inc. doesn't handle them for you.

## How much to ask for

File for **at most $100 USD**.

The cap limits what you file, not what you can be paid. Maintainers may adjust the final amount up or down, and the adjusted amount can be more than $100. Don't track hours and don't try to work out what you "should" get. We pay for the value and scope of merged, reviewed work, not for time spent. Hours are easy to inflate and impossible to verify, so we don't use them.

Please don't treat the number you file as the number you'll receive. If the final amount differs from your claim, that's the process working as designed.

## Fixed budget

The monthly budget is fixed. If claims for a month add up to more than the budget, amounts are scaled down to fit.

## When you get paid

We pay by international wire every second Friday after an expense is approved.

Each approved expense is paid as its own wire. We don't combine expenses into one wire. The fee rule below depends on the approved (adjusted) amount of that one expense, not on what you filed:

- **Under $500 USD:** we cover all wire fees, so you receive the full amount.
- **$500 USD and above:** we send with shared charges, so your bank may deduct a fee.

You can see the status on your expense page. Please don't ping maintainers for updates, the page is up to date.

## Transparency

Payments are public on the Open Collective ledger: who was paid, how much, and when. Your bank details and tax documents stay private and are visible only to the people who need them to pay you.

## Abuse policy

An expense without verifiable evidence of the work is rejected.

- **First time:** fix it and resubmit.
- **Second time:** approvers may leave the expense closed and unpaid, or adjust it.
- **After that:** the submitter may be barred from filing further expenses.

If something about this process is confusing, unfair, or not working for you or for the project, tell us. Use Discord or email hari@observal.io. We'll change what needs changing. We ask that you stay open to changes too, since the budget and the process are both still young.
